05 PERPLEXITY EXPLORER

Model Uncertainty & Sequence Perplexity

How surprised is an LLM by your text? Connects directly from Decoder Logits → Softmax Token Probabilities → Sequence Perplexity (PPL) using client-side DistilGPT-2 or interactive simulation.

1. Decoder Softmax
\(P(x_i \mid x_{\lt i})\) P(xi | x<i)
Probability distribution over entire vocabulary from causal logits
2. Actual Token Probability
\(P_i = P(\text{target}_i)\) Pi = P(targeti)
Exact probability value assigned to the ground-truth token
3. Surprise & Loss
\(S_i = \frac{1}{P_i} \;\Longleftrightarrow\; \text{NLL}_i = -\ln(P_i)\) Surprise = 1 / Pi  |  Loss = -ln(Pi)
Rarer tokens yield higher surprise (1/Pi) and higher cross-entropy loss
4. Geometric Mean (PPL)
\(\text{PPL} = \exp\left(-\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N \ln P_i\right)\) PPL = exp(-1/N × ∑ ln Pi)
Equivalent branching factor: effective choices per token
Intuitive meaning of Perplexity: A perplexity of \(K\) means the language model was, on average across the entire text, as uncertain as if it were choosing uniformly among \(K\) equally likely words at each position. Lower perplexity means higher model confidence and natural flow.
